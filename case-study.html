The challenge

One marketplace. Two distinct ambitions.

Professionals needed a credible path to opportunity, while businesses needed a fast way to find specialised talent and manage work with confidence.

The experience had to make both audiences feel understood without fragmenting the product into two unrelated platforms.

Our response

Make every next step obvious.

02

Clear routes help people explore categories, find expertise, understand the offer and move confidently toward action.

03

04

The core journeys were designed to remain focused and legible across mobile and desktop contexts.
